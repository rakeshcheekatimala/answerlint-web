# Brand voice policy — v1

Assess five separate dimensions:

1. Accuracy: observed statements do not conflict with approved claims.
2. Completeness: high-stakes buyer requirements are not omitted.
3. Differentiation: the brand's supported distinction is visible without hype.
4. Trust: important claims are backed by appropriate owned or independent sources.
5. Risk: inaccurate, outdated, legally sensitive or ambiguous statements are explicit.

Use `insufficient_evidence` whenever the supplied package cannot support a
judgment. Never improve the intended voice by inventing marketing claims.
